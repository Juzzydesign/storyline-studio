# ICP Interview: Malu

## Background
- Approximately 8 years of experience.
- Actively job searching.

## Primary goal
Improve employment opportunities and professional visibility.

## Challenges
- Networking and visibility are significant concerns.
- Difficulty accessing opportunities through professional connections.
- Portfolio improvements do not appear to be her primary problem.

## Behaviour and attempted solutions
- Paid for career coaching.
- Evaluated a more expensive coaching package.
- Declined a package costing approximately $2,000.

## Competitive alternatives
- Career coaching.
- Networking and relationship building.
- Professional visibility activities.

## Evidence of willingness to pay
- Has paid for career coaching.
- Rejected a higher-cost package.
- No demonstrated demand for Storyline's portfolio intensive.

## Desired outcome
- Better access to opportunities.
- Improved professional visibility.
- More effective career development.

## Storyline relevance
Weaker fit for a narrowly portfolio-focused offer.

## Important uncertainties
- Specific coaching outcomes and purchase motivations require further investigation.
- Do not assume that portfolio positioning would solve her primary challenges.

## Research takeaway
Not every experienced designer struggling to find employment needs portfolio help.

This is an important negative case for Storyline's ICP definition.
