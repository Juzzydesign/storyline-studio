# ICP Interview: Josh

## Background
- More than 20 years of experience.
- Previously worked at Amazon and Microsoft.
- Recently laid off.
- Balancing career transition with family responsibilities.

## Primary goal
Find an effective and sustainable route back into employment.

## Challenges
- Uncertainty about which job-search activities deserve attention.
- Difficulty knowing how much portfolio work is sufficient.
- Questions about whether to prioritise networking, portfolio revisions, speculative projects or content creation.
- Limited time and competing personal responsibilities.

## Behaviour and attempted solutions
- Evaluated different job-search approaches.
- Considered multiple activities without certainty about their likely value.

## Competitive alternatives
- Networking.
- Portfolio development.
- Speculative projects.
- Content creation.
- Other job-search activities.

## Evidence of willingness to pay
- No confirmed spending or buying intent from the available interview summary.

## Desired outcome
- Clear priorities.
- Confidence that time is being spent on the right activities.
- A manageable approach to the job search.

## Storyline relevance
Potential fit for prioritisation and career strategy, but not necessarily for a portfolio-focused offer.

## Important uncertainties
- No established evidence that his portfolio is the main obstacle.
- Unclear whether a portfolio workshop would address his most urgent need.

## Research takeaway
For some experienced designers, the problem is deciding where to invest limited time rather than improving any single career asset.
