# ICP Interview: Weena

## Interview date
October 27, 2026

## Background
- Experienced designer.
- Background in physical product design and gaming.
- Interested in transitioning into other industries, including fintech and healthcare.
- Exploring opportunities in Switzerland.
- Seeking professional growth, new skills and improved compensation.

## Primary goal
Transition into a different industry and secure a suitable role.

## Challenges

### Transferable experience
- Unsure whether employers will value experience from another industry.
- Believes some employers prefer candidates with direct domain experience.
- Questions how to make existing skills relevant to different roles.

### Portfolio decisions
- Unsure whether she needs separate case studies for each industry.
- Has considered creating numerous case studies tailored to different opportunities.
- Questions how much portfolio work is enough.
- Struggles to determine what to prioritise.

### Lack of progress
- Has not been receiving the interview results she wants.
- Portfolio analytics suggest that people may not be visiting her portfolio.
- This makes it uncertain whether portfolio content is actually the problem.

## Behaviour and attempted solutions
- Used AI to revise CV materials.
- Used AI to critique portfolio content.
- Reviewed approximately 20–30 other portfolios for inspiration.
- Considered creating a speculative banking chatbot project.
- Repeatedly questioned what changes to make.
- Previously paid for general career coaching.

## Competitive alternatives
- AI tools.
- General career coaching.
- Portfolio inspiration and comparisons.
- Independent case study development.
- Speculative projects.

## Experience with paid coaching
- Paid multiple general career coaches.
- Received confidence-building and general job-search advice.
- Found the guidance insufficiently specific to design.
- Wanted more actionable recommendations and clearer diagnosis.

## Evidence of willingness to pay
- Demonstrated spending on career coaching.
- No confirmed willingness to purchase Storyline's services.
- Previous spending suggests interest in professional support, not necessarily in a portfolio workshop.

## Desired outcome
- Understand how to communicate transferable skills.
- Identify which existing projects are relevant to target industries.
- Know whether new projects are necessary.
- Make fewer unnecessary portfolio changes.
- See examples of designers who successfully transitioned industries.

## Storyline relevance
Strong potential problem fit for role-specific positioning and evidence mapping.

## Important uncertainties
- Lack of portfolio visits means portfolio presentation may not explain the lack of interviews.
- Swiss hiring requirements, local market conditions, visa considerations and application strategy may contribute.
- No evidence that she would purchase a group intensive.

## Research takeaway
Experienced designers transitioning industries may struggle to distinguish between skills they already possess and the evidence needed to communicate those skills to a new audience.
