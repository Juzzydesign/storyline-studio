# ICP Interview: Kalyani

## Background
- Approximately 2 years of design experience.
- Junior designer and career switcher.
- Actively applying for opportunities.

## Primary goal
Secure employment and develop professionally.

## Challenges
- Limited application success.
- Questions about presenting work affected by NDA restrictions.
- Uncertainty about demonstrating AI-related skills.
- Challenges associated with limited professional experience.

## Behaviour and attempted solutions
- Applied for multiple opportunities.
- Sought information about portfolio presentation.
- Previously paid for career-related training.

## Competitive alternatives
- Career training.
- Independent learning.
- Portfolio guidance and educational resources.

## Evidence of willingness to pay
- Has previously purchased career-related training.
- No confirmed willingness to pay for Storyline's current offers.

## Desired outcome
- Communicate existing skills more effectively.
- Understand how to demonstrate relevant capabilities.
- Improve opportunities for interviews.

## Storyline relevance
Potential fit for educational resources, but outside Storyline's current primary mid-level and senior audience.

## Important uncertainties
- Junior hiring challenges may differ substantially from experienced-designer positioning problems.
- Previous educational spending does not validate demand for this particular workshop.

## Research takeaway
Career switchers and junior designers may share some portfolio concerns with experienced designers, but should not automatically be grouped into the same ICP.
