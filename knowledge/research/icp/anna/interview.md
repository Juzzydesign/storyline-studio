# ICP Interview: Anna

## Background
- Design Systems designer working as a contractor at Disney.
- Seeking a permanent remote Design Systems role.
- Approximately 150 applications with limited interview progress.

## Primary goal
Move into a permanent Design Systems position.

## Challenges
- Difficulty obtaining feedback relevant to Design Systems roles.
- Generic career and portfolio advice does not necessarily address specialist requirements.
- Uncertainty about how to position her experience for target opportunities.

## Behaviour and attempted solutions
- Paid approximately $70–80 for a mentoring session.
- Found the feedback too generic.
- Purchased Brad Frost's AI and Design Systems course.
- Continued seeking relevant professional development and guidance.

## Competitive alternatives
- General design mentors.
- Specialist educational courses.
- Independent portfolio improvement.

## Evidence of willingness to pay
- Has purchased mentoring and educational products.
- Demonstrated willingness to invest in professional development.
- No confirmed willingness to pay for Storyline's intensive.

## Desired outcome
- Specialist feedback relevant to Design Systems hiring.
- Clear guidance on communicating relevant experience.
- More useful recommendations than generic portfolio advice.

## Storyline relevance
Potential fit for specialised portfolio reviews and Design Systems career positioning.

## Important uncertainties
- Limited interview progression cannot automatically be attributed to portfolio quality.
- Purchasing a technical course does not establish demand for portfolio coaching.

## Research takeaway
Specialists may be dissatisfied with generic mentoring when it fails to reflect the expectations of their discipline.
