# ICP Interview: Mike

## Interview date
October 30, 2026

## Background
- Experienced designer.
- Background in Design Systems, strategy and accessibility.
- Previously worked at a large company.
- Currently unemployed.
- Has family responsibilities and needs to manage expenses.

## Primary goal
Get more interviews and secure employment.

## Job-search progress
- Hundreds of job applications.
- Approximately seven interviews.
- Three progressed to second rounds.
- Experienced recruiter ghosting and limited feedback.
- In one hiring process, interviewers suggested he was overqualified for the original role.

## Challenges

### Uncertainty about portfolio effectiveness
- Believes his work and case study stories are strong.
- Unsure whether presentation and discoverability are effective.
- Questions whether hiring managers see relevant evidence.
- Does not know which improvements will make a difference.

### Conflicting advice
- Has received contradictory recommendations about case study quantity.
- Unsure whether to demonstrate breadth or specialise.
- Unsure whether to create additional case studies.
- Wants to demonstrate visual design alongside systems and strategy experience.

### Feedback quality
- Has asked friends and former colleagues for feedback.
- Feels they may avoid being sufficiently critical.
- Wants independent, honest, specific feedback.
- Values professional expertise and evidence of successful outcomes.

### NDA restrictions
- Can explain the stories behind his work.
- Has difficulty accessing or sharing supporting visual assets.
- May need to reconstruct some visuals.
- Had not considered asking his previous employer for permission to use specific material.

## Behaviour and attempted solutions
- Continually makes portfolio adjustments.
- Reviews successful portfolios.
- Experiments with approaches used by other designers.
- Uses AI to critique portfolio content.
- Monitors portfolio traffic.
- Develops additional case studies.
- Has attended free educational sessions.
- Is actively researching paid mentoring options.

## Competitive alternatives
- Free peer feedback.
- AI-generated portfolio feedback.
- Independent revisions.
- Sarah Doody's career programmes.
- MentorCruise.
- Individual portfolio mentoring.

## Evidence of willingness to pay
- Considered programmes costing approximately $2,500 or more.
- Rejected these as too expensive and financially risky.
- Said he could spend up to approximately $300 on an individual session.
- Is actively comparing paid mentoring options.
- Has not confirmed purchasing a service.

## Desired outcome
- Understand what is working in his portfolio.
- Identify what is not working and why.
- Determine whether hiring managers see the evidence they need.
- Receive concrete recommendations for improvement.
- Stop relying on guesswork.

## What he values in a service
- Independent expert feedback.
- Specific critique rather than general reassurance.
- Explanations behind recommendations.
- Evidence that the mentor has helped other designers.
- Manageable financial risk.
- A focused individual engagement.

## Storyline relevance
Strong potential fit for an expert portfolio diagnosis or focused 1:1 review.

His stated preference may be better aligned with an individual service than a group workshop.

## Important uncertainties
- The portfolio may not be the primary cause of his job-search difficulties.
- His stated $300 comfort level is not a purchase commitment.
- He has not demonstrated demand for Storyline's existing intensive.

## Research takeaway
Some experienced designers move toward paid professional feedback after exhausting free alternatives.

The immediate value they seek may be a diagnosis of what needs improving, rather than a comprehensive educational programme.
