# ICP Interview: Edison

## Background
- Approximately 8 years of experience.
- Background in B2B SaaS and Design Systems.
- Actively searching for employment.
- Approximately 80 applications, 30 interviews, 3 final rounds and 1 offer.

## Primary goal
Secure a role that reflects his experience and seniority.

## Challenges
- Difficulty communicating seniority through case studies.
- Uncertainty about how to demonstrate impact and individual contribution.
- Repeatedly revising portfolio content without knowing whether it is improving.
- Difficulty determining which aspects of his experience hiring teams value.

## Behaviour and attempted solutions
- Revised case studies approximately 5–6 times.
- Sought specialist mentoring.
- Paid for four mentoring sessions.
- Continued making changes to improve how his experience was presented.

## Competitive alternatives
- Independent portfolio revisions.
- Specialist mentoring and feedback.

## Evidence of willingness to pay
- Has already paid for specialist mentoring.
- Actual spending is stronger evidence than hypothetical willingness to pay.
- No confirmed purchase of Storyline.

## Desired outcome
- Communicate seniority and contribution more effectively.
- Understand what to prioritise in case studies.
- Feel confident that his experience is being represented appropriately.

## Storyline relevance
Strong potential fit for role-specific portfolio positioning, evidence selection and case study feedback.

## Important uncertainties
- It is not established that portfolio presentation was the reason for interview rejections.
- We do not know whether he would purchase a group workshop instead of individual mentoring.

## Research takeaway
Experienced designers may repeatedly revise portfolios when they lack confidence about which evidence demonstrates seniority.
